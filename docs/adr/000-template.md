# ADR-NNN: Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What's going on that forces a decision? Constraints, numbers, the people affected.
Keep it factual. Someone reading this in a year shouldn't need the Slack thread.

## Decision

What we're doing, stated plainly.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, and what we'd have to do to reverse it.

## Alternatives considered

The options we didn't pick, and the one-line reason why.
