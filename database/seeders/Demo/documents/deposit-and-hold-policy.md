# Deposit and Vehicle Hold Policy (S-110)

Holding a vehicle takes it off the market, so we only do it with a deposit and for a fixed time.

## Holding a vehicle

A refundable deposit of $500 holds a vehicle for 72 hours. During the hold the vehicle is marked "On hold" in inventory and cannot be sold to another customer.

Without a deposit, a sales manager may approve a courtesy hold of up to 24 hours, for example while a spouse comes in to see the vehicle. Reps cannot approve courtesy holds themselves.

## When the deposit becomes non-refundable

The deposit stays fully refundable until the customer signs the bill of sale. Once the bill of sale is signed and financing is approved, the deposit is applied to the purchase and is no longer refundable.

If financing is declined, the full deposit is refunded within 5 business days to the original payment method.

## Accepted payment for deposits

Deposits can be paid by debit, credit card or e-transfer. We do not accept cash deposits over $500. Card details are never written down or stored in the CRM. Payments go through the payment terminal only.

## Extending a hold

A hold can be extended once, by another 72 hours, with sales manager approval and a note in the lead's timeline explaining why.
