# Test Drive Process (S-105)

## Before the drive

Every driver must show a valid full G licence (or out-of-province equivalent). Take a photo of the licence with the dealership tablet and attach it to the lead. Drivers with a G2 licence may test drive only with a fully licensed passenger aged 20 or older who has held a G licence for at least 4 years.

Check that the vehicle has a dealer plate, at least a quarter tank of fuel (or 40% charge for an EV), and no warning lights.

## The route

The standard route takes about 30 minutes and includes the 401 on-ramp so customers can feel highway merging and road noise. For trucks, offer the gravel lot behind the service bay. For family buyers, bring car seats from the showroom so they can test the fit.

The rep always rides along on a standard test drive.

## Overnight test drives

Hot leads in the Negotiation stage can take a vehicle home overnight for up to 24 hours with sales manager approval. The customer signs the overnight agreement, their insurance details are recorded, and the vehicle must be back by 10 a.m. the next day. Overnight test drives are not available on vehicles under $25,000 or on new models in their first month of release.

## After the drive

Log the test drive in the lead's timeline the same day, including what the customer liked and any objections, and move the lead to the Test drive stage.
