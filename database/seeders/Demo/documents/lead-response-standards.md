# Lead Response Standards (S-100)

How quickly we respond is the single biggest factor in whether an internet lead buys from us.

## Response times

- Website, marketplace and live chat leads: first response within 15 minutes during business hours
- Leads that arrive after hours: first response by 10 a.m. the next business day
- Phone and walk-in leads: logged in the CRM before the end of the same day

## The BDC's role

The business development centre (BDC) reviews every unassigned lead by 10 a.m. each business day and assigns it to a rep, balancing by each rep's open lead count.

## Follow-up cadence

If the customer hasn't responded, make contact attempts on day 1, day 2, day 4 and day 7, alternating between phone, email and text according to their preferred contact method. After day 7 with no response, mark the lead Cold and move to a monthly check-in.

A lead with no customer contact (call, email, text or meeting) for more than 3 days counts as stale and appears on the manager's morning stale-lead list. Private notes don't count as contact.

## Consent

Only send marketing emails or texts to customers who have given express consent, in line with Canada's anti-spam legislation (CASL). Replies to a customer's own enquiry are fine. Record consent in the lead's timeline.

## Business hours

Monday to Thursday 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., Friday and Saturday 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., closed Sunday.
