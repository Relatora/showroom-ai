# Northgate Shield Extended Warranty Plans (W-120)

Northgate Shield plans start when the manufacturer's warranty ends (or on delivery for used vehicles) and are backed by our service department.

## Plans and prices

| Plan          | Coverage                                                                              | Term                 | Price  |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ------ |
| Powertrain    | Engine, transmission, drive axles, hybrid and EV drive units                          | 5 years / 100,000 km | $1,895 |
| Comprehensive | Powertrain plus electrical, air conditioning, steering, suspension, infotainment      | 4 years / 80,000 km  | $2,795 |
| Platinum      | Everything in Comprehensive plus seals, gaskets, roadside assistance and a rental car | 7 years / 160,000 km | $3,895 |

## Deductible

Every plan has a $100 deductible per repair visit, no matter how many items are fixed during that visit.

## Hybrid and EV batteries

High-voltage batteries are already covered by the manufacturer for 8 years or 160,000 km. Northgate Shield does not duplicate this coverage, and reps must not sell any plan as "battery protection".

## Transfers and cancellation

A plan can be transferred to a new private owner once, for a $50 fee. A customer can cancel within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. After 30 days, refunds are pro-rated by time or kilometres used, whichever is greater, minus a $75 administration fee.

## What isn't covered

Wear items (brakes, tires, wiper blades, bulbs), cosmetic damage, and any damage from accidents, flooding, racing or modifications.
