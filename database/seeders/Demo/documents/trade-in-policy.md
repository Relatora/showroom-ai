# Trade-in Appraisal Policy (F-204)

Northgate Motors appraises every trade-in the same way, so customers get a consistent, defensible number no matter which rep they work with.

## Appraisal timing

Every trade-in is appraised by the used-car manager or a certified appraiser within 24 hours of the vehicle arriving on the lot. Reps may give a "ballpark" range on the phone, but it must be described as an estimate until the vehicle has been inspected in person.

## How long an offer is valid

A written trade-in offer is valid for 7 days or 500 additional kilometres, whichever comes first. After that the vehicle must be re-appraised. Offers are tied to the condition at appraisal: new damage, warning lights or missing equipment void the offer.

## Older and high-mileage vehicles

Vehicles older than 12 model years, or with more than 250,000 km, are wholesale only. We do not retail them on our lot. The offer is based on auction value and the customer should be told this up front so the number doesn't come as a surprise.

## Liens and payouts

If the trade-in has a loan or lease on it, the customer must provide a payout letter from the lender dated within the last 10 days. Northgate pays out the lender directly on delivery day.

Negative equity (owing more than the vehicle is worth) can be rolled into the new financing up to a maximum of $5,000, subject to lender approval. Anything above $5,000 must be paid down by the customer.

## What the customer needs to bring

- Vehicle ownership (green slip) in the customer's name
- Both sets of keys and any key fobs
- Owner's manual and service records, if available
- Payout letter, if there is a lien

Missing second keys reduce the offer by $300 to cover replacement.
