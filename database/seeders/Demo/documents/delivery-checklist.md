# Vehicle Delivery Checklist (S-130)

A good delivery takes about 90 minutes and sets up the next sale. Book it as a Delivery appointment in the CRM.

## Before the customer arrives

- Vehicle detailed, fuelled to full (or charged to 80% for an EV), and plates installed
- Customer's insurance confirmation (pink slip) received and attached to the deal
- Trade-in payout confirmed with the lender, if there is one
- Any accessories (winter tires, mats, roof racks) installed and checked

## With the customer

1. Walk around the vehicle together and note anything they want fixed before they leave
2. Pair their phone and set up Apple CarPlay or Android Auto
3. Show the driver assistance features: adaptive cruise, lane-keeping, blind-spot monitoring
4. For EVs, show charging, preconditioning and the charge-port release
5. Introduce them to the service advisor and book the first maintenance visit

## After delivery

Call the customer 3 days after delivery to answer any questions, and again at 30 days to ask for a review and referrals. Log both calls in the timeline and move the lead to Won if it isn't already.

Customers who refer a friend who buys from us receive a $250 service credit.
