# Finance and Leasing Guide (F-300)

Rates change monthly. This guide reflects the current month's programs. Always confirm the final rate with the finance office before quoting it in writing.

## Promotional rates on new vehicles

- Toyota: 3.99% for up to 60 months on RAV4 Hybrid, Highlander Hybrid and Sienna
- Honda: 4.49% for up to 60 months on CR-V Hybrid and Pilot
- Hyundai and Kia: 2.99% for up to 48 months on all electrified models (Ioniq 5, Tucson Hybrid, Santa Fe Hybrid, Sorento PHEV, EV9)
- Ford: 0.99% for up to 36 months on F-150 Lightning and Mustang Mach-E

All other new vehicles are financed at the lender's standard rate, typically 5.49% to 6.99%.

## Used and certified pre-owned

Used vehicle rates depend on the customer's credit tier:

- Tier 1 (excellent credit): from 6.99%
- Tier 2 (good credit): from 7.99%
- Tier 3 (rebuilding credit): from 9.99%

Certified pre-owned vehicles qualify for 1 percentage point off the tier rate.

## Terms and down payments

Terms run from 24 to 84 months. Used vehicles older than 7 model years are limited to 60 months and require a minimum 10% down payment.

## Co-signers

A co-signer is accepted for first-time buyers and customers in Tier 3. The co-signer must attend the signing in person with government photo ID.

## Leasing

Leases are available on new vehicles only, for 24, 36 or 48 months, with annual kilometre allowances of 16,000, 20,000 or 24,000 km. Excess kilometres are charged at $0.12 per km at the end of the lease.
