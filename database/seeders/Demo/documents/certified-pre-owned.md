# Certified Pre-Owned Program (W-200)

Certified pre-owned (CPO) vehicles sit between new and used: inspected to a higher standard and backed by our own warranty.

## Eligibility

To be certified, a vehicle must be less than 6 model years old, have fewer than 120,000 km, and have a clean history report with no structural damage, flood damage or salvage title.

## The inspection

Every CPO vehicle passes a 160-point inspection by a licensed technician. Tires must have at least 6/32 inch of tread remaining and brake pads at least 50% of their original thickness. Anything below that is replaced before certification.

## Warranty

CPO vehicles come with 12 months or 20,000 km of comprehensive coverage, plus the remainder of any manufacturer powertrain warranty. This can be upgraded to any Northgate Shield plan at a 15% discount.

## 30-day exchange

If the customer isn't happy, they can exchange the vehicle for another CPO vehicle of equal or greater value within 30 days or 2,000 km, whichever comes first. The exchange is available once per purchase and the vehicle must be in the same condition as when it was sold.

## Finance benefit

CPO vehicles qualify for 1 percentage point off the customer's used-vehicle credit tier rate (see the Finance and Leasing Guide, F-300).
