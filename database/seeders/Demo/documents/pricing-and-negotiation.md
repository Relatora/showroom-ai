# Pricing and Negotiation Guidelines (S-120)

## All-in pricing

Ontario's OMVIC rules require all-in advertised pricing. The advertised price must include freight, PDI and our $799 administration fee. Only HST and licensing are added on top. Never quote a price that leaves out fees the customer will have to pay.

## Discount authority

- Sales reps may discount up to $500 off the advertised price without approval
- Sales managers may approve discounts up to $2,000 on new vehicles and $1,500 on used vehicles
- Anything larger needs the general manager

## Aged inventory

Vehicles that have been on the lot for more than 60 days carry an extra $1,000 of manager discretion on top of the normal limits. These units are flagged in inventory, and moving them is a priority.

## Price matching

We will consider matching a written quote from another Ontario dealer on the same new model, trim and options, if the quote is less than 7 days old. The sales manager makes the final call.

## Add-ons

Never pressure customers into add-ons. Extended warranties, rustproofing and tire packages are offered once, explained clearly, and recorded in the timeline whether the customer accepts or declines.
